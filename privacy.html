Bubblify Privacy Policy

Bubblify is a free answer-sheet tool for teachers. It runs in your browser. Last updated October 2026.

What Bubblify stores

The classes, student names and ID numbers, quizzes, answer keys, scanned answers, scores, and small cropped images of handwritten names that you enter or scan. This data is saved in your own browser's storage on your device. Photos and camera images are processed on your device and are not uploaded anywhere by Bubblify.

Google sign-in and Google Drive (optional)

If you choose "Connect and sync", Bubblify asks Google for two permissions: access to its own private app-data folder in your Google Drive, and your email address (to show which account is connected). Bubblify uses the app-data folder only to save and restore a backup of your Bubblify data so you can use it on more than one device. It cannot see or change your other Drive files. Your data goes directly between your device and your own Google Drive.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The developer of Bubblify does not receive, collect, store, sell, or share your data or your Google account information. There are no ads, analytics, or tracking. The only outside script Bubblify loads is Google's sign-in library, and only when you use sync.

Bubblify's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Student information

If you enter student names or IDs, you are responsible for handling them according to your school's rules. Bubblify keeps them only on your device and in your own Drive.

Deleting your data

Use "Clear all data" in Bubblify's Settings, or clear your browser's site data, to remove local data. Use "Disconnect" in Settings to stop syncing. To delete the Drive backup, open your Google Account, go to Third-party apps and services, choose Bubblify, and delete its data.

Contact

Questions: contact the developer of Bubblify at the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
